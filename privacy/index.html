In short

  • Beetario has no registration and no login. There are no user accounts, profiles or advertising with us.
  • Gardens, plants, photos, journal, beds, reminders and settings are stored on your device.
  • Data only leaves the device for functions you use. Identification and AI answers only run after your consent.
  • Only rounded coordinates (about 1 km) are sent, never your address and no background location.

Data on your device

Your gardens, plants, photos, journal entries, beds, tasks, reminders and settings are stored only in the app on your device. Photos are stored without location data. You can export everything as ZIP, CSV or PDF and save a backup via «Save as». If you delete the app or its data, this data is deleted; export it first.

What our server stores

The server (Google Firebase, region Zurich) knows no name and no e-mail address. Firebase creates an anonymous ID for the app the first time it is used. Linked to this ID we store:

  • the quota counters for AI questions and identifications,
  • the result of the purchase check (level Pro or AI-Plus and time of the check), not the payment data,
  • a daily limit for feedback.

Without reference to a person we store: the weather cache per rounded coordinate (expires automatically), counters «helpful / not helpful» per plant card, counters of scientific names that were looked for without a card (for choosing new cards) and the daily total of Pl@ntNet requests. Questions, answers and photos are passed on but not stored on our server. Technical server logs (with the anonymous ID, without content) are kept by Google Cloud Logging for about 30 days.

For the retention at the services themselves, their own terms apply (for example Pl@ntNet keeps a request history according to its terms).

Deleting data

  • On the device: delete individual entries in the app, or delete the app data or uninstall the app.
  • Google Drive (sync): delete the Beetario files in your Drive and remove the access in your Google account under «Third-party apps and services».
  • On our server: the anonymous ID with quota, purchase level and feedback limit contains no name and no garden data. After deleting the app data or reinstalling, the app receives a new anonymous ID and the old entry is no longer used. If you want us to delete the entry, write to hello@beetario.ch. Because the entry contains no name, we need a clue to find it; we will reply with the procedure.
  • Purchases: your purchase stays with Google Play and can be restored there.

Consent

Identification and AI questions only run after your consent. Without consent, catalogue, watering plan, reminders, bed planner and journal keep working. You can withdraw the consent at any time in the settings.

Legal basis

The revised Swiss Data Protection Act (revDSG/FADP) applies, and for users in the EU and EEA the GDPR. Legal bases under the GDPR: providing the app functions you use (Art. 6(1)(b)), your consent for identification and AI (Art. 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in a secure, misuse-free server with quotas and logs (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Transfers abroad

Data goes to Norway (EEA) and France (EU), which both offer adequate data protection. Image requests, climate data, terrain tiles and some Google services run in the USA. Google LLC and Amazon Web Services are certified under the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; for the rest only rounded coordinates or plain image requests are sent, never garden data.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). To deliver the pages, GitHub processes your IP address in server logs. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no external fonts or scripts.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object. Because your data is on your device, you exercise most of these rights directly there (export, delete). For everything else write to hello@beetario.ch. You can lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, Switzerland) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

United Kingdom and United States

United Kingdom: the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

United States (including California): Beetario does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising («sale» and «sharing» within the meaning of the CCPA/CPRA). There is no account or profile with us; access and deletion concern the data on your device and, on request, the entries on the server (hello@beetario.ch).

Changes

We update this policy when the app changes what leaves the device. The date at the top shows the current version.
