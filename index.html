How it works

Three steps, then the app does the remembering. No sign-up, no account.

  1. 1

    Set up your garden

    Enter a place or postcode, then add spots: room, balcony, bed, raised bed or pot. That way the app knows the weather, frost dates, light and soil.

  2. 2

    Add plants

    Identify by photo or search the catalogue of over 8,000 species. More than 300 of them have a plant card with sourced details such as light, water and toxicity.

  3. 3

    Check «Today»

    The «Today» page shows watering, tasks and frost warnings. Tap «Watered» or «Not needed», and the watering plan learns from it.

What the garden planner app does

Six jobs Beetario takes off your hands through the gardening year. The pictures are screenshots from the app with a sample garden.

Identify plants from a photo

Not sure what is growing in your bed? Take a photo of a leaf or flower. Identification is done by Pl@ntNet, a project of French research institutes. You get three suggestions with a match score and decide for yourself which one is right.

If none fits, pick the plant from the catalogue or add it by hand. The photo is sent without location data, and identification only runs after you consent. You can identify up to five plants a day.

A watering plan with weather: sometimes «no need»

The watering plan is the heart of the app. Outdoors it keeps a water balance: rain and evaporation from the MET Norway forecast, plus the soil and the plant's root depth. If 12 mm of rain fell two days ago, it says: no need. The calculation is right below, so you can follow it.

Indoors the watering plan starts from the plant card and adjusts for season, light and pot. Tap «Still moist» or «Already dry» to correct it, and it learns. The amount is shown as a range, not a falsely precise figure. Numbers always come from the calculation, never from the AI. For outdoor beds the watering plan is marked beta in its first season.

Frost warnings for outdoor plants

When a cold night is coming, Beetario shows a frost warning when you open it: how cold it will get and how many of your outdoor plants need protection. The app knows from your garden what is outside. It warns you about heat as well.

It can work out your garden's frost dates from climate data for your location, or you enter your own. Sowing and harvest times on the plant cards move with them. To get the warning as a notification while the app is closed, allow the battery exception once.

Bed planner with companion planting and crop rotation

In the bed planner you lay out a raised bed, vegetable patch or window box as a grid and place your plants. Below it, companion planting shows which neighbours support each other and which are unfavourable. Companion planting is experience, not a rule, so it says «without warranty».

For next year you switch to 2027 and reuse the old plan as a template. If the same plant family ends up in the same spot again, the bed planner flags it as a crop rotation hint. As a PDF, the plan can hang in the shed.

Ask questions, get answers with sources

Why does my ficus have yellow leaves? Beetario answers questions like this with what you have already saved: plant card, spot, the last 30 days of the journal and the weather. Every answer shows what it is based on, for example «Plant card: Ficus benjamina, light» and «Journal: watered 2× in 14 days».

If that is not enough, you can start a web search, and it is labelled as such. Answers come from Google Gemini and only run after you consent. Beetario does not recommend sprays; for treatment, ask a garden centre.

Holiday cover as a PDF

Before the summer holidays you create a PDF for whoever waters for you: spot by spot, every plant with a photo, where it stands in words, the amount such as «about 1.5–2.2 litres» and boxes to tick off for each day. The finger test is explained at the top.

No address and no GPS on it. Email the PDF or print it, and your plants get through the holidays in good shape.

Questions about the garden planner app

Is the garden planner app free?

Yes. The free tier has no plant limit and no ads: plant cards, journal, reminders and the interval-based watering plan. Pro is a one-time purchase and unlocks the watering plan with weather, the frost warning, the bed planner for all beds, the pruning module and the PDF exports.

How does Beetario know when I need to water?

Outdoors from a water balance with rain and evaporation from the forecast, the soil and the root depth. Indoors from the plant card, adjusted for season, light and pot. Your feedback «still moist» and «already dry» makes the watering plan more accurate.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and get started. If you use several devices, Pro can sync through your own Google Drive; the files are then in your Drive, not with us.

Which phones does Beetario run on?

Android 8.0 or newer. There is currently no iPhone version.

Can I use °F and inches?

Yes. Under Settings, Display you choose metric or US units and a light or dark theme. The app always calculates in metric and only converts for display.

Why doesn't Beetario recommend sprays?

Plant protection products are regulated differently in every country. Beetario names possible causes and signs, but no products and no amounts. For treatment, ask a garden centre or your regional plant protection service.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, create a backup file under Backup, copy it to the new one and choose «Restore» there. With Pro, the Google Drive sync does this for you.
