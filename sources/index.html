Sources

Beetario computes with open data and names its sources. In the app, every field of a plant card shows its source, and each picture shows its author and licence.

Plant cards

  • RHS (Royal Horticultural Society): Plant Finder, «Month by Month» and «Potentially harmful plants».
  • NC State Extension: Extension Gardener Plant Toolbox.
  • PFAF (Plants For A Future).
  • WCVP, World Checklist of Vascular Plants, Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew (CC BY 4.0): accepted names, synonyms, families.
  • Wikidata (CC0) and GBIF for the basic cards.
  • Wikimedia Commons: card pictures under free licences only (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA); author and licence are shown in the app.
  • BAFU (Swiss Federal Office for the Environment): invasive alien species.

Watering

Evaporation after FAO-56: Allen, R. G., Pereira, L. S., Raes, D. & Smith, M. (1998): Crop evapotranspiration. FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 (Penman-Monteith, crop coefficients).

Weather

Data from MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Climate data for frost dates

These data were obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center (LaRC) POWER Project funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science Program.

Identification and AI

Identify with Pl@ntNet (Pl@ntNet consortium: Cirad, INRAE, Inria, IRD, with the Agropolis Foundation). Comparison images from Pl@ntNet are shown with author and licence. AI answers and photo diagnosis: Google Gemini, labelled as such in the app.
