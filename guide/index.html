Garden guides: plan, care, prune

Short how-tos for the gardening year, each with its sources: how to plan your beds, protect plants from late frost and prune shrubs and trees at the right time.

Every guide starts with the short answer and then goes into detail. Timings and amounts come from bodies such as the Royal Horticultural Society, NABU or national weather services, and every page lists its sources. Where experts disagree, we say so.

The guides are written for home gardens, allotments, balconies and houseplants, not for farming or commercial growing. We do not recommend pesticides.
